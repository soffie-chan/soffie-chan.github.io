THE PROTOTYPE

The game ‘Monkey Business’ is a tabletop game that consists of three players attempting to fix a model together. However, one of the players cannot hear, one cannot see, and one cannot talk. The mute player has a deck of cards, each card consisting of various colored blocks and pieces that are oriented in different ways. After drawing one, the mute player attempts to explain the type of block and other properties of the model on the card to the player that cannot hear by using only their bodies. The deaf player further tries to interpret the mute player’s signs to guide the blind player, who is responsible for assembling the model together from nothing by feeling each block. As the hands of all three players are hidden behind boards, the mute player cannot see the assembly as the blind player fixes it. Once the players think they have completed the model, they lift their boards to reveal how well they replicated the model on the card. This can be a challenge for 3 players to figure out how to repair a model from the ground up, or a competition between 6 players to find the team that cooperates best in a 3v3.
